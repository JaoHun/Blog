# Images

Use `incoming` as a temporary intake folder. Move images to a stable folder before referencing them in posts or config.

- `avatar`: author avatar or personal images.
- `moments`: travel photos, life notes, and photo essay images.
- `og`: default Open Graph and social preview images.
- `posts`: article images, preferably grouped by post slug.
- `projects`: project screenshots and preview images.
- `incoming`: unsorted images waiting for review.

For travel photos or life records, move reviewed images from `incoming` to `moments/` before publishing. Use names such as `2024-11-17-bipenggou-cover.jpg` and keep public images compressed before committing.
